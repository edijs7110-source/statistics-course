Known issues, according to the shift supervisor:

- Times in the batch log are local plant time, which changed to summer time on 29 March.
- Somebody thinks a batch was entered twice. Nobody remembers which one.
- Suppliers are the same companies as in March: see suppliers.csv from Module 1.
