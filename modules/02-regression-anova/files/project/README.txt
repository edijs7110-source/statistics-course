R3 COMMISSIONING RUNS: HANDOVER NOTE
From: Dana (process engineering)   To: the statistics team

Attached: r3_runs.csv, 64 commissioning runs of the new 2,000 L reactor R3,
1 September to 12 October 2026.

Columns
  run_id          C01 to C64
  run_date        date of the run
  catalyst        A (standard) or B (new; costs more, see below)
  feed_water_ppm  water content of the feed, measured by the lab for each run
  temp_C          average reactor temperature during the run, deg C
  residence_min   residence time, minutes
  stir_rpm        agitator speed. R3's agitator runs from 110 to 230 rpm
  yield_pct       yield, percent of theoretical
  purity_pct      product purity, percent (the customer's limit is 98.5)
  notes           anything the operators wrote down

The question
  Production on R3 starts in November. Which temperature, residence time and
  agitator speed should we run at, to get the highest yield while keeping
  purity at or above 98.5%? What yield can we promise, and how sure are we?

  Catalyst B costs more. It's worth it if it adds at least 2 percentage points
  of yield. Does it?

Known issues
  - On the pilot reactors, agitator speed made no difference. R3 is ten times
    bigger, so please check that again.
  - The run sheets were typed in by hand. I haven't checked them.

Thanks! Dana
